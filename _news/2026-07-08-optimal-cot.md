New preprint: [The Optimal Sample Complexity of Learning Autoregressive Chain-of-Thought](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07423). In the realizable PAC setting, exact-trace learning achieves the standard multiclass sample-complexity rate of the local next-token class, independent of rollout length.
